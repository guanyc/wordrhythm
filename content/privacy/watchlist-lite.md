# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies only to the mobile app identified below.

The identifiers in this policy should match the Google Play store listing for this app, including the app name, developer name, legal entity, and package name.

**WatchList Tracker Lite** (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Watchlist Data (Local Storage Only)
The App stores your stock codes, watchlists, action and reason tags, daily notes, execution status, analysis views, and language preference locally on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no upload of this data to us.

The App also includes backup and export tools that write a copy of this data — as JSON, XML, or a database file — to a location you pick on your own device. You control that file completely; it is never sent to us.

If Android's system backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may include the App's local data in your own Google backup. That backup is configured and controlled by you in your device settings, not by us.

### b) Advertising
The App uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Ads allow us to offer the App free of charge.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain information to request, serve, and measure ads, including:

*   Advertising ID (e.g., GAID)
*   **Approximate (coarse) location**, derived from sources such as your IP address or nearby network information
*   Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
*   IP address
*   Ad impressions, clicks, and other interaction data

The App declares the advertising-ID and approximate-location permissions that the ads SDK requires. The App itself never asks you for your location and does not use it for any other purpose.

Ads may be **personalized** or **non-personalized**, in accordance with Google's policies and your consent choices (managed via Google's **User Messaging Platform** where required).

For more information, please visit: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### c) No Analytics or Crash Reporting
This build carries the **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Crashlytics** libraries, but both collection switches are turned off in the app's manifest. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent: no usage statistics, no device logs, and no crash reports.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

*   Save and display your watchlist, notes, tags, and preferences on your device.
*   Display advertisements that keep the App free.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we never receive your watchlist or notes.

Information may be processed by one third-party service provider:

*   **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads):** for advertising services.

AdMob operates under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Your Choices & Controls

We believe you should control your data:

*   **Advertising privacy:** where required (for example in the EEA or the UK), the App shows a Google consent form before requesting ads, and your choice can be changed later. All users can reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in device settings (*Settings → Google → Ads*).
*   **Local data:** the backup and export tools are the only way your data leaves the App, and only to a location you choose. Uninstalling the App deletes its local database.
*   **No purchases:** this App has no in-app purchases, so nothing is charged beyond what Google Play itself applies to the download.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user data.

*   Your watchlist data is stored in the App's private sandbox and is not transmitted to us.
*   Ad requests and any related data are transmitted over HTTPS.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is a tracking and journaling tool for investors and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
