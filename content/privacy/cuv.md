# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies only to the mobile app identified below.

The identifiers in this policy should match the Google Play store listing for this app, including the app name, developer name, legal entity, and package name.

**CUV Bible** (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Reading Data (Local Storage & Backup)
The App stores reading progress, bookmarks, favorites, notes, and user preferences locally on your device.

If you choose to enable backup, this data is saved to your personal Google Drive account using the Google Drive AppData folder (`drive.appdata` scope).

**The App:**
*   Only accesses its own AppData folder.
*   Cannot view or access other files in your Google Drive.
*   Does not modify any unrelated files.

Backup data remains under your control within your Google account.

### b) Analytics & Crash Reporting
The App may use **Google Firebase** to improve stability and user experience.

*   **Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics):** May collect anonymous data about how you interact with the app.
*   **Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** May automatically collect anonymous reports when the app crashes.

If these features are available in the app, they should match the disclosures shown in Google Play Data safety and in-app settings.

### c) Advertising
The App may use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

If ads are enabled, AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve ads, including:
*   Advertising ID
*   Approximate location
*   Device information
*   IP address
*   Usage data

This information is used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements in accordance with Google’s policies and your consent choices.

For more information, please visit: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### d) In-App Purchases
The App may use **Google Play Billing** to process in-app purchases.
We do not collect or store payment information directly. All transactions are securely handled by Google Play.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:
*   Save your reading progress and preferences.
*   Enable backup and restore functionality.
*   Display advertisements, if enabled.
*   Process in-app purchases, if offered.
*   Analyze usage trends to improve app features.
*   Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes to maintain app stability.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the app build you install, including:
*   **Google AdMob:** For advertising services.
*   **Google Firebase:** For analytics and crash reporting.
*   **Google Drive:** For user-initiated backup.
*   **Google Play Billing:** For purchase processing.

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Your Choices & Controls

The App may provide the following controls, depending on the features enabled in the published build:

*   **Usage Analytics:** Toggle On/Off in app settings, where available.
*   **Crash Reports:** Toggle On/Off in app settings, where available.
*   **Advertising Privacy:** Manage ad personalization through in-app privacy options or device settings, where applicable.
*   **Google Drive Access:** Revoke the App's access to Drive at any time via your Google Account permissions.
*   **Local Data:** Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user data.
*   Local data is stored in the app's private sandbox.
*   Backup data is stored securely within your Google Drive AppData folder and is not publicly accessible.
*   Analytics and crash data are transmitted securely and are anonymized where applicable.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the Effective Date at the top of this policy.
