# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies only to the mobile app identified below.

The identifiers in this policy should match the Google Play store listing for this app, including the app name, developer name, legal entity, and package name.

**Yorùbá + KJV** (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

The App carries two translations: the Open Yoruba Contemporary Bible (OYCB, © Biblica, Inc., used under CC BY-SA 4.0) and the King James Version (public domain). Both texts ship inside the App, so reading a verse never sends it to us or to anyone else.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Reading Data (Local Storage & Backup)
The App stores your reading data and preferences locally on your device. This includes the display mode you chose (Yorùbá, KJV, Yorùbá first, or KJV first), reading position and history, search history, highlights, bookmarks, favourites, notes, tags, memorization progress, reading-plan progress, and app settings.

If you sign in with Google and turn backup on, this data is saved to your personal Google Drive account using the Google Drive AppData folder (`drive.appdata` scope). Backup is off by default. An optional weekly automatic backup runs only after you enable it, and it waits for Wi-Fi before sending anything.

**The App:**

*   Only accesses its own AppData folder.
*   Cannot view or access other files in your Google Drive.
*   Does not modify any unrelated files.

The backup file contains your reading data and settings. It does not contain the Bible text, which is already part of the App. Backup data remains under your control within your Google account. If you never enable backup, none of this data leaves your device.

### b) Analytics & Crash Reporting
The App uses **Google Firebase** to improve stability and the reading experience.

*   **Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics):** Collects anonymous data about how you use the app (for example which screens and features you open). This helps us understand usage trends and improve the app.
*   **Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** Automatically collects anonymous reports when the app crashes, including stack traces and device state information, to help us fix bugs.

Both can be turned off in **Settings → Other** (“Usage analytics” and “Crash reports”). With them off, the App keeps working exactly as before.

### c) Advertising
The App uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. Ads allow us to offer the App for free.

AdMob may automatically collect certain information — in both current and future versions of the App — to serve and measure ads, including:

*   Advertising ID (e.g., GAID)
*   **Approximate (coarse) location**, derived from sources such as your IP address or nearby network information
*   Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
*   IP address
*   App usage and interaction data

Future versions of the App may continue to share coarse (approximate) location with AdMob, alongside the information listed above. We update this policy and the app’s Google Play Data safety disclosure before any new category of information is collected.

This information is used to provide **personalized** or **non-personalized** advertisements in accordance with Google’s policies and your consent choices (managed via Google’s **User Messaging Platform**, and changeable at any time under **Settings → Privacy Options**).

For more information, please visit: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### d) In-App Purchases
Where the App offers in-app purchases (for example “Remove Ads” or a subscription), they are processed by **Google Play Billing**.

We do not collect or store payment information. All transactions are securely handled by Google Play.

### e) Device Permissions
The App asks for a permission only when a feature needs it:

*   **Microphone:** voice search, and the soundtrack of a verse video you record yourself. Speech recognition is handled by the speech service on your device; a video you record stays on your device until you choose to share it.
*   **Camera:** recording a verse video. It is used only while you are recording, and only if you start that feature.
*   **Notifications:** reading-plan reminders and the verse of the day. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification content is pushed from our servers.
*   **Foreground media playback:** keeps audio playing when the screen is off.
*   **Approximate location:** the App itself never asks you for your location. The advertising SDK declares an approximate-location permission; any approximate location AdMob receives is derived from your IP address and is used only to serve ads, as described above.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

*   Save your reading progress, display mode and preferences.
*   Enable backup and restore functionality (only if you turn backup on).
*   Display advertisements (to support the free version).
*   Process in-app purchases.
*   **Analyze usage trends** to improve app features.
*   **Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes** to maintain app stability.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers:

*   **Google AdMob:** For advertising services.
*   **Google Firebase:** For analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).
*   **Google Drive:** For user-initiated backup (data remains in your Google account).
*   **Google Play Billing:** For purchase processing.

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.

One exception is not a third party at all: when you share a verse, the Yorùbá text travels with the attribution required by its licence (Biblica® Open Yoruba Contemporary Bible™ © 2009, 2017 Biblica, Inc., CC BY-SA 4.0). Whatever you send, you send yourself, to an app you picked.

## 4. Your Choices & Controls

We believe you should control your data:

*   **Usage Analytics / Crash Reports:** Toggle on or off in *Settings → Other*.
*   **Advertising Privacy:**
    *   **GDPR/UK users:** Use *Settings → Privacy Options* to manage consent for ad personalization.
    *   **All users:** Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your device system settings (*Settings → Google → Ads*).
*   **Google Drive access:** Revoke the App’s Drive access at any time through your Google Account permissions.
*   **Search history:** Clear it from the search screen at any time.
*   **Local data:** Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user data.

*   Local data is stored in the app’s private sandbox.
*   Backup data is stored in your own Google Drive AppData folder and is not publicly accessible.
*   Analytics and crash data are transmitted securely (HTTPS) and are anonymized.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
