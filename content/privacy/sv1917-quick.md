# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies only to the mobile app identified below.

The identifiers in this policy should match the Google Play store listing for this app, including the app name, developer name, legal entity, and package name.

**Svenska 1917 Bible quick** (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Reading Data (on your device)
The App stores the Bible text and your reading data locally on your device: reading position, favorites, bookmarks, notes, highlights, tags, memorization state, reading-plan progress, statistics, and preferences.

Search history and recently opened passages are also kept locally so the locator and search screens can show them back to you.

There is **no in-app cloud backup or cloud sync**. Your reading data is not uploaded by the App. Android's own device backup may still apply according to your device settings and Google account configuration.

### b) Analytics & Crash Reporting
The App can use **Google Firebase** to improve stability and user experience. Both features are **off by default**.

* **Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics):** anonymous information about screens visited and features used, such as feature events and truncated search terms (up to 24 characters).
* **Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** anonymous crash reports, including stack traces and basic device information such as manufacturer, model, Android version and app version.

You choose whether to enable each feature in **Settings**, and you can turn either one off again at any time. When disabled, no analytics or crash data is collected for that category.

### c) Advertising
The App may show **Google AdMob** banner ads to support free distribution. Before ads are requested, the App asks for consent where required through Google's **User Messaging Platform**.

AdMob may process information such as:

* Advertising ID (GAID)
* Approximate (coarse) location derived from network information
* Device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size
* IP address
* App interactions with ads

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent choices. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### d) Text-to-Speech
Text-to-speech uses the Android system TTS engine and optional user-selected voice. The App does not record your voice and does not send Scripture text to a server of its own.

### e) In-App Purchases
The App does not process payments or store payment information.

## 2. How We Use Information

Local reading data is used only to provide the reading experience: remembering where you were, showing your notes and marks, and building statistics.

Analytics and crash data, when you enable them, are used to understand usage trends and fix stability problems. Advertising data is used by Google to select and measure ads.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers:

* **Google AdMob:** advertising and consent handling.
* **Google Firebase:** analytics and crash reporting, only if you enable them.
* **Google Play:** app distribution, in-app review and Android backup infrastructure.

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Your Choices & Controls

* **Analytics and crash reports:** enable or disable independently in *Settings*. Both start disabled.
* **Advertising privacy:** use the “Privacy Options” entry in Settings where available, or reset / limit ad personalization in your device's Google settings.
* **Local data:** uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data. Android backup copies, if any, are managed through your Google account.
* **Language:** the interface can be switched between Swedish and English; this only changes display language, not stored tag keys or reading data.

## 5. Data Security

Local data is stored in the app's private sandbox. Network communication with Google services is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable technical measures to protect user data, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
