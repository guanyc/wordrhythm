# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies only to the mobile app identified below.

The identifiers in this policy should match the Google Play store listing for this app, including the app name, developer name, legal entity, and package name.

**Littafi Mai Tsarki (Hausa)** (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

The App carries one translation: the Biblica® Open Hausa Contemporary Bible™ (© 2020 Biblica, Inc., used under CC BY-SA 4.0), all 66 books and 31,087 verses. The text ships inside the App, so reading a verse never sends it to us or to anyone else. The interface is available in Hausa and in English.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Reading Data (Local Storage)
The App stores your reading data and preferences locally on your device. This includes your reading position and history, search history, highlights, bookmarks, favourites, notes, tags, memorisation progress, reading-plan progress, saved devotionals and your daily streak, and app settings.

This data stays in the App’s private storage. The App has no account and no cloud sync: it does not ask you to sign in, and nothing you read, highlight, or note is uploaded to us. There is no Google Drive integration — a class for it exists in the code but it is not part of the App and cannot be reached.

If you choose **Export**, the App writes a backup file (JSON) through the system file picker to a location you choose. You can import that file again later. The file is yours; deleting the App or the file deletes it.

### b) Analytics & Crash Reporting (Opt-In)
The App uses **Google Firebase** to improve stability and the reading experience. Both are **off by default** and stay off until you agree:

*   **Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics):** Collects anonymous data about how you use the app (for example which screens and features you open). This helps us understand usage trends and improve the app.
*   **Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** Collects anonymous reports when the app crashes, including stack traces and device state information, to help us fix bugs.

On first launch the App asks for your consent; nothing is reported unless you allow it. Both can be turned off again at any time in **Settings → Game da** (“Ƙididdigar amfani” and “Rahotannin kuskure”). With them off, the App keeps working exactly as before.

### c) Advertising
The App is free and uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. Only one format is used: a **banner** on the welcome screen, in search, in the book locator and on the reading-plan lists. No ad of any kind appears on the Bible reading screen, in your favourites, in your bookmarks or among your search results.

AdMob may automatically collect certain information — in both current and future versions of the App — to serve and measure ads, including:

*   Advertising ID (e.g., GAID)
*   **Approximate (coarse) location**, derived from sources such as your IP address or nearby network information
*   Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
*   IP address
*   App usage and interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)

This information is used to provide **personalized** or **non-personalized** advertisements in accordance with Google’s policies and your consent choices (managed via Google’s **User Messaging Platform**). Where Google requires a consent choice — for readers in the EEA and the UK — **Settings → Game da → Zaɓuɓɓukan sirri** reopens that form and lets you change or withdraw your choice at any time.

For more information, please visit: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### d) In-App Purchases
The App offers **no in-app purchases** and no subscription: it is free to read, supported by ads. Every feature is unlocked. If purchases are introduced in a future version, they will be processed by **Google Play Billing**; we do not collect or store payment information, and this policy will be updated first.

### e) Device Permissions
The App asks for a permission only when a feature needs it:

*   **Storage / files:** saving or restoring your backup file, through the system file picker.
*   **Notifications:** devotional and reading-plan reminders. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification content is pushed from our servers.
*   **Internet and network state:** loading ads and, only if you opt in, sending analytics and crash reports.
*   **Advertising ID:** used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
*   **Approximate location:** the App declares this permission because the AdMob SDK may use it to serve ads. The App itself never asks for your location and has no location feature.

The App declares **no microphone and no camera permission**. Voice search does not record anything itself: it hands a request to the speech recogniser installed on your phone, and that service handles the audio under its own terms. Read-aloud likewise uses the speech engine on your device; no audio is downloaded or uploaded by the App.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

*   Save your reading progress and preferences.
*   Restore your data when you choose to import a backup file.
*   Display advertisements (to support the free version).
*   **Analyze usage trends** to improve app features (only if you opt in).
*   **Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes** to maintain app stability (only if you opt in).

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers:

*   **Google AdMob:** For advertising services.
*   **Google Firebase:** For analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), only after you consent.

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.

Reading the Bible, searching, and saving notes never sends anything to a third party. The App sends no reading data anywhere.

When you share a verse, the verse text, the app’s name and the translation credit travel — and only to the app you picked. The App does not upload it. The credit reads Biblica® Open Hausa Contemporary Bible™, © 2020 Biblica, Inc., CC BY-SA 4.0, and it is also shown inside the App, in **Settings → Game da → Fassara da haƙƙin mallaka**. If you publish the text elsewhere, that licence asks you to keep the credit with it.

## 4. Your Choices & Controls

We believe you should control your data:

*   **Usage Analytics / Crash Reports:** Off until you agree; toggle on or off any time in *Settings → Game da*.
*   **Advertising Privacy:**
    *   **GDPR/UK users:** Use *Settings → Game da → Zaɓuɓɓukan sirri* to review or withdraw consent for ad personalization.
    *   **All users:** Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your device system settings (*Settings → Google → Ads*).
*   **Search history:** Clear it from the search screen at any time.
*   **Backup file:** Delete the file from wherever you saved it whenever you like.
*   **Local data:** Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user data.

*   Local data is stored in the app’s private sandbox.
*   A backup file is written only to the location you pick, and is not uploaded by the App.
*   Analytics and crash data are transmitted securely (HTTPS) and are anonymized.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
