# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies only to the mobile app identified below.

The identifiers in this policy should match the Google Play store listing for this app, including the app name, developer name, legal entity, and package name.

**Word Rhythm: Swahili Bible** (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

The App carries one translation: the Biblica Toleo Wazi Neno: Bibilia Takatifu (ONEN, the Open Kiswahili Contemporary Version, © 1984, 1989, 2009, 2015 Biblica, Inc., used under CC BY-SA 4.0). The text ships inside the App, so reading a verse never sends it to us or to anyone else. The interface is Kiswahili throughout.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Reading Data (Local Storage)
The App stores your reading data and preferences locally on your device. This includes your reading position and history, search history, highlights, bookmarks, favourites, notes, tags, memorisation progress, reading-plan progress, downloaded audio, and app settings.

This data stays in the App’s private storage. The App has no account and no cloud sync: it does not ask you to sign in, and nothing you read, highlight, or note is uploaded to us.

If you choose **Backup**, the App writes a copy of that data to a file in a folder on your own device, which you can restore from later. That file is yours; deleting the App or the folder deletes it.

### b) Analytics & Crash Reporting (Opt-In)
The App uses **Google Firebase** to improve stability and the reading experience. Both are **off by default** and stay off until you agree:

*   **Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics):** Collects anonymous data about how you use the app (for example which screens and features you open). This helps us understand usage trends and improve the app.
*   **Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** Collects anonymous reports when the app crashes, including stack traces and device state information, to help us fix bugs.

On first launch the App asks for your consent; nothing is reported unless you allow it. Both can be turned off again at any time in **Settings → Mipangilio mingine** (“Washa uchanganuzi wa matumizi” and “Washa ripoti za hitilafu”). With them off, the App keeps working exactly as before.

### c) Advertising
The App is free and uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements (banner and native ads). Ads allow us to offer the App for free.

AdMob may automatically collect certain information — in both current and future versions of the App — to serve and measure ads, including:

*   Advertising ID (e.g., GAID)
*   **Approximate (coarse) location**, derived from sources such as your IP address or nearby network information
*   Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
*   IP address
*   App usage and interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)

Future versions of the App may continue to share coarse (approximate) location with AdMob, alongside the information listed above. We update this policy and the app’s Google Play Data safety disclosure before any new category of information is collected.

This information is used to provide **personalized** or **non-personalized** advertisements in accordance with Google’s policies and your consent choices (managed via Google’s **User Messaging Platform**). Where Google requires a consent choice — for readers in the EEA and the UK — **Settings → Chaguo za faragha** reopens that form and lets you change or withdraw your choice at any time.

For more information, please visit: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### d) In-App Purchases
The App offers **no in-app purchases** and no subscription: it is free to read, supported by ads. Every feature, including the full set of reading plans, is unlocked. If purchases are introduced in a future version, they will be processed by **Google Play Billing**; we do not collect or store payment information, and this policy will be updated first.

### e) Device Permissions
The App asks for a permission only when a feature needs it:

*   **Microphone:** voice search, and the soundtrack of a verse video you record yourself. Speech recognition is handled by the speech service on your device; a video you record stays on your device until you choose to share it.
*   **Camera:** recording a verse video. It is used only while you are recording, and only if you start that feature. Sharing a verse as an image card uses the system picker and needs no camera access.
*   **Storage / files:** saving or restoring your backup file.
*   **Notifications:** devotional and reading-plan reminders. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification content is pushed from our servers.
*   **Location:** the App itself never asks for your location and declares no location permission. Any approximate location AdMob receives is derived from your IP address and is used only to serve ads, as described above.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

*   Save your reading progress and preferences.
*   Restore your data when you choose to restore from a backup file.
*   Display advertisements (to support the free version).
*   **Analyze usage trends** to improve app features (only if you opt in).
*   **Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes** to maintain app stability (only if you opt in).

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers:

*   **Google AdMob:** For advertising services.
*   **Google Firebase:** For analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), only after you consent.

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.

Reading the Bible, searching, and saving notes never sends anything to a third party. Read-aloud uses the speech service on your device, and the App sends no reading data anywhere.

One exception is not a third party at all: when you share a verse, the Kiswahili text travels with the attribution required by its licence (Biblica® Toleo Wazi Neno: Bibilia Takatifu™ © 1984, 1989, 2009, 2015 Biblica, Inc., CC BY-SA 4.0). Whatever you send, you send yourself, to an app you picked.

## 4. Your Choices & Controls

We believe you should control your data:

*   **Usage Analytics / Crash Reports:** Off until you agree; toggle on or off any time in *Settings → Mipangilio mingine*.
*   **Advertising Privacy:**
    *   **GDPR/UK users:** Use *Settings → Chaguo za faragha* to review or withdraw consent for ad personalization.
    *   **All users:** Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your device system settings (*Settings → Google → Ads*).
*   **Search history:** Clear it from the search screen at any time.
*   **Backup file:** Delete the file from the folder on your device whenever you like.
*   **Local data:** Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user data.

*   Local data is stored in the app’s private sandbox.
*   A backup file is written to storage you control, and is not uploaded by the App.
*   Analytics and crash data are transmitted securely (HTTPS) and are anonymized.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
